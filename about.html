ABOUT THE DOCTOR

Dr. Deepak Koppaka

Consultant Medical Oncologist

Dr. Deepak Koppaka is a Consultant Medical Oncologist in Hyderabad with academic and clinical training in both Radiation Oncology and Medical Oncology.

His academic journey includes MBBS from G.S.L. Medical College, Rajahmundry, MD in Radiation Oncology from the Postgraduate Institute of Medical Education and Research (PGIMER), Chandigarh, and DM in Medical Oncology from Kidwai Cancer Institute, Bangalore.

His professional work includes the evaluation and treatment of solid tumours and haematological malignancies, with an emphasis on evidence-based systemic cancer therapy, multidisciplinary planning and individualised treatment decisions.

Alongside clinical practice, Dr. Deepak Koppaka has been involved in oncology research, presentations and peer-reviewed publications across lung cancer, breast cancer, haematological malignancies and other areas of oncology.
